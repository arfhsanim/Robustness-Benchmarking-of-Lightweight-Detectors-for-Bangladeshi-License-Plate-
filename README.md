# Robustness Benchmarking of Lightweight Detectors for Bangladeshi License Plate Detection Under Adverse Weather Conditions

— This paper benchmarks the robustness of lightweight object detectors for Bangladeshi license plate detection under rain, fog, and low-light degradation. Three strategies built around YOLOv8-nano are compared — a baseline trained on clean images, classical CLAHE+Gamma restoration applied at inference, and weather-augmented retraining — together with a cross-architecture comparison against YOLOv5-nano and MobileNet-SSD. Under heavy fog, baseline mAP@50 drops from 0.978 (clean) to 0.876. Classical restoration recovers roughly 30% of this loss (0.891) at zero training cost but adds 12.3 ms of inference latency, reducing throughput from 45.2 to 35.8 FPS; weather-augmented training recovers nearly all of it (0.951) with no inference overhead. We quantify the resulting accuracy–costlatency trade-off and offer a practitioner decision framework for edge deployment.
